# About

## Project

**Analysis and Conceptualization of Analog Layout Techniques**

Master’s thesis in Communication Electronics at the Technical University of Munich.

The project studies how manually developed analog layout knowledge can be evaluated, represented, and reused in a structure-aware automatic physical-design flow.

The work combines:

- manual analog layout;
- quantitative and comparative evaluation;
- layout-knowledge abstraction;
- reusable analog layout generation;
- physical-design representation;
- B*-tree-based placement;
- well-domain handling;
- hierarchical assembly;
- access-aware routing.

## Thesis Structure

This portfolio follows the central research path of the thesis:

**manual study → conceptual framework → automation-oriented representation
→ reusable generation → hierarchical physical integration**

## Research Interests

- Analog layout automation
- Electronic design automation
- Analog physical-design methodology
- Layout-aware circuit optimization
- Open-source integrated-circuit design flows
